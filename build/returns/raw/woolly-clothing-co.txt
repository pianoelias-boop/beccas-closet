 Refund policy
 Returns & Warranty 
 We want you to love what you order, but we know sizing and color don't always work out — so returns are easy. Unworn items in original condition (tags on, returned in the original Woolly envelope) can be returned within 30 days of delivery, underwear included. Closeout items are final sale.
 When you start a return, choose an exchange, Shop Now credit, store credit, or refund. Exchanges and store credit ship free; refunds have an $8 return shipping fee. Refunds process within 5 business days of your item being scanned by the carrier; store credit is issued immediately.
 Depending on your location, you'll either get a QR code for a box-free, label-free drop-off, or a printed shipping label — instructions are provided when you complete your return.
 Warranty: Every Woolly product is backed for a year from purchase. If an item arrives defective or develops a problem with normal wear, file a claim through our warranty portal — no need to send the item back, and there's never a fee. Most claims are reviewed within 24 hours.
 Questions? Email help@woolly.clothing .
