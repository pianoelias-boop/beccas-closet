 Refund policy
 What is your return policy? 
 We make returns and exchanges easy by providing you with a mailing label for only $5.
  *Please note that the customer is responsible for returns shipping costs and duties for international orders (excluding Canada).
 How can I make a return or exchange? 
 Please visit our   returns portal   to begin your return or exchange process. Your reference number begins with IBX or EXC-IBX (if you are returning an exchange).
 What is your "Shop Now" feature? 
 Our returns page also allows you to "Shop Now" instead of returning for a full refund or store credit. If you select our Show Now feature, you will also be given a $10 credit but the credit will only be given if 1) you create a new order right then and there on our returns site and 2) your new order total exceeds the amount of the original purchase. **If your new order total does not exceed the amount of the original purchase OR you use less than the $10 credit, you are not owed any additional refunds or credit. You must use the entire $10 credit in that new order to receive it.
 Can I return for store credit instead? 
 Yes, you have the option to return your order for either a full refund back to the original form of payment or for store credit in form of an e-gift card that will be emailed to you.
 Do I have to pay for my return label? 
 Returns and exchanges do require a $5 handling fee.
  *Please note that the customer is responsible for returns shipping costs and duties for international orders (excluding Canada).
 Can I use my own return label to send back my return? 
 No. If you go to the post office and send us back your items without going throgh our returns portal, we will have no way of knowing and you will not receive your refund or exchange. Our system is all automatic and you must go through the returns portal to do so. Please only use the pre-paid UPS return label we provide.
 I have two separate orders I'm returning. Can I combine them into one package to save on costs? 
 Please do not combine different orders into one package / return label. If returning multiples orders at once, please follow the steps for each order # and ship them separately using their respective pre-paid labels provided.
 How can I track my exchange order status? 
 One your return has been accepted back to our warehouse, your exchange order will ship and you will receiving a shippig notification via email with your new tracking number.
  Are any items on your website not eligible for a return? 
 Yes. Any items that are altered, washed, worn, or damaged will not be eligible for refund or credit unless approved by our team.
 Any item marked as final sale will not be accepted for a refund.
 Our Face Masks are not eligible for returns due to safety precautions.
 My item became damaged after one wear/wash. Can I return for a replacment?  
 Though rare, if you find that your item is damaged after one wear or wash, please email us at hello@ibex.com with your IBX order number, which product is damaged and an explanation of what the damage is, as well as photos of the product. We will come up with a solution to ensure your satisfaction.
 When will my return be processed? 
 Please allow 5-7 business days for our warehouse to inspect your return and process your refund or exchange.
