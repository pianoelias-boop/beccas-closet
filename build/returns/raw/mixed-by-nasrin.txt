 Refund policy
 Returns & Exchanges
 We want you to love what you ordered. If something isn't right, here's how returns and exchanges work.
 Return Window
 You may return or exchange eligible items within 14 days of the delivery date . Orders that include Order Protection are eligible for an extended 30-day return window (see below).
 Condition Requirements
 To be eligible for a return or exchange, items must be:
 Unworn and unwashed
 Free of any damage, stains, odor, or signs of wear
 Returned with all original tags attached
 We reserve the right to decline any return that does not meet these conditions.
 Exchanges
 We offer free exchanges within the return window. If you'd like a different size, color, or style, you can start an exchange through our returns portal.
 Return Fee
 Standard returns are subject to a $10 fee , which covers the cost of your prepaid return shipping label. This fee is deducted from your refund.
 For customers who purchased Order Protection, this $10 fee is waived, and return shipping is on us.
 Final Sale
 Items marked Final Sale are not eligible for return or exchange. This will be clearly indicated on the product page and in your cart at checkout.
 How to Start a Return or Exchange
 Visit our returns portal to begin:
 https://returns.mixedbynasrin.com 
 You'll be guided through selecting your items and, if applicable, generating a prepaid return label. Once we receive and inspect your returned item(s), your refund or exchange will be processed.
 Order Protection
 Order Protection is an optional service you can add at checkout to improve your post-purchase experience. When added to your order, it includes:
 Free returns & exchanges - the standard $10 return fee is waived, and we cover return shipping.
 Extended 30-day returns - your return window is upgraded from 14 days to a full 30 days from the delivery date.
 Priority handling of shipping issues - if your order is lost in transit, arrives damaged, or doesn't reach you, we prioritize resolving it, including expedited replacement or reshipment at our discretion.
 Order Protection is an optional service, not an insurance product. Resolution of shipping issues is handled at our discretion in accordance with this policy. Order Protection does not alter our delivery terms above: once a carrier marks an order as delivered, Mixed is not responsible for packages lost or stolen after delivery, though we will always do our best to help where we can.
 Order Protection does not change item eligibility. Items marked Final Sale, Re-Mixed pieces, and any other items designated as final sale remain non-returnable and non-exchangeable, even when Order Protection is purchased. The free returns & exchanges and extended 30-day return window apply only to items that are otherwise eligible under this policy. Orders containing final sale or Re-Mixed items still receive Order Protection's other benefits, including priority handling of shipping issues.
 Mixed reserves the right to modify, suspend, or discontinue Order Protection at any time. Any changes apply only to future orders - if you purchased Order Protection, you will continue to receive the benefits as described at the time of your purchase.
 Questions?
 For any questions reach out to us at hello@mixedbynasrin.com and our team will be happy to help.
