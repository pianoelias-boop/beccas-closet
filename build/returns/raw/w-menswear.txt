 Shipping & Returns
 FAQ
 What currency are your products priced in?
 All products on our site are priced in U.S. Dollars (USD).
 How do you ship?
 All orders are shipped from our design studio in Bangkok, Thailand.
 Thailand Orders
 Domestic orders are shipped via EMS Express and typically arrive within 1 business day.
 International Orders
 International orders are shipped via DHL Express and typically arrive within 3–5 business days from the shipping date.
 We offer a flat-rate shipping fee worldwide. Orders over $200 USD qualify for complimentary shipping.
 Please note that local duties, customs fees, and import taxes may apply depending on your country’s regulations. These charges are the responsibility of the customer.
 How do I know my size?
 Our garments generally run from Women’s XS–XL unless otherwise noted.
 We recommend referring to the detailed measurements provided on each product page to find your best fit. If you have any questions, feel free to email us at info@wmenswear.com — we’re always happy to help.
 Returns & Exchanges
 Please contact W’MENSWEAR at info@wmenswear.com before returning any items.
 Thailand Returns
 Thailand-based customers may return eligible items within 14 days of delivery for a refund or exchange.
 Returned items must be:
 unworn
 unwashed
 in original condition
 with all original tags attached
 Items that are damaged, worn, altered, or returned without tags cannot be accepted.
 International Returns & Exchanges
 International orders are eligible for exchange only.
 Eligible exchange requests must be made within 10 days of delivery. If delivery has been postponed at the customer’s request, eligible exchanges must be initiated within 20 days of the shipment date.
 Customers are responsible for:
 return shipping costs
 customs fees
 import duties associated with returned parcels
 Please note that international returns may incur customs charges of up to 30% of the product value, which will be deducted from any eligible refund or exchange processing.
 To request an exchange, please email info@wmenswear.com with:
 your order number
 the item you wish to return
 the item and/or size you would like instead
 If you would like sizing guidance before exchanging, we’re happy to help.
 Sale & Pre-Order Items
 All sale and pre-order purchases are final sale and cannot be returned or exchanged.
 No additional price adjustments will be made on sale items.
 Return Shipping Guidelines
 We recommend using a tracked shipping service and retaining your proof of postage until your return has been processed.
 Please clearly mark all international return parcels as:
 “RETURNED GOODS”
 W’MENSWEAR cannot be held responsible for returned packages lost in transit without proof of delivery.
 Transport Damage
 If your order arrives damaged, or if your parcel is lost during transit, please contact us immediately at info@wmenswear.com .
 Unclaimed Packages
 If a parcel is unclaimed or refused by the customer, W’MENSWEAR reserves the right to deduct any return shipping fees, customs charges, and administrative costs incurred.
