 Refund policy
 30-DAY SATISFACTION GUARANTEE
 If our woolen apparel does not perform to your satisfaction, return it to the store from which you bought it within 30 days for a replacement or a refund. You must include your dated purchase receipt.
 If you are unsatisfied with your purchase beyond 30 days, we still want to know how we can fix it. For non-warranty satisfaction returns, please  contact us  as soon as possible via email or phone to discuss a solution and include your original purchase receipt.
 RETURN INSTRUCTIONS
 All returns must be authorized. 
  Please call us at 1-855-646-8733 for authorization or submit the form below.
 Please allow time for a Minus33 customer service rep to approve your return. If you submit using the form below, a confirmation email will be sent within 24 business hours after it has been approved. 
 CONDITIONS FOR ALL RETURNS AND WARRANTY CLAIMS:
 You are responsible for all freight charges to return product to us.
 Do not continue to wear your product if you plan to return it. Heavily worn items can interfere with our ability to properly inspect your product.
 We reserve the right to charge a cleaning fee for dirty/soiled products. This includes products covered with pet hair. Please wash your products if they are dirty before sending them back.
 We reserve the right to charge a restocking fee on any unworn, unwashed products that are sent back to us without the original tags and packaging.
 All returns are subject to inspection and the outcome will be determined on a case-by-case basis.
 Returns should be postmarked within 15 days of authorization.
 PLEASE INCLUDE:
 Items to be returned
 Proof of purchase required to receive a refund. Items without a receipt can not be guaranteed to receive the desired result.
 Original packaging materials/tags if possible.
 SHIP ITEMS TO:
 via US Postal Service: 
 via UPS or FedEx: 
 Minus33 ATTN: RETURNS
 PO BOX 515 
Ashland, NH 03217 
 Minus33 ATTN: RETURNS
 22 Mill Street 
Ashland, NH 03217 
 GIFTS
 Please fill out this form if you would like to exchange this item for something else if you did not purchase this item yourself. 
 Gifts that are unwashed/unworn without a receipt can only be exchanged for an item of equal value, and are subject to inspection. A copy of the receipt is preferred when possible.  Gift Exchange 
 INTERNATIONAL RETURNS
 International returns/exchanges must be held responsible for all shipping, duties, and taxes for both ways and are not recommended. If you feel your purchase is eligible for a warranty claim, please contact us to discuss a solution.
 European Union 14 day cooling off period 
Notwithstanding the above, if the merchandise is being shipped into the European Union, you have the right to cancel or return your order within 14 days, for any reason and without a justification. As above, your item must be in the same condition that you received it, unworn or unused, with tags, and in its original packaging. You’ll also need the receipt or proof of purchase.
 PROCESSING TIME
 Please allow 3-4 weeks for shipping time, inspection, and processing. Most returns will happen faster, but please be patient and consider the transit time from your location to New Hampshire. Any refunds will be credited back to the original purchasing method. Please also allow additional processing time during and directly following the holiday season. Thank you for your patience.
