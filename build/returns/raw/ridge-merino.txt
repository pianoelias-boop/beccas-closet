 Refund policy
 Return Policy 
 Ridge offers free returns and exchanges for U.S. (domestic) orders within 90 days of purchase.
 Returns must be submitted accurately and adhere to these conditions to be eligible:
 Return must be submitted within 90 days of purchase.
 Items must be returned in new condition, unworn (other than tried for fit), unwashed, undamaged and clean.
 Items must be free of pet and human hair, stains (including deodorant), dirt, etc.
 Items must be returned in their original packaging and include original hang tags.
 Invoice (order) number must be included in return package.
 Items returned in poor condition will not be eligible for refund, store credit or exchange. Ridge Merino reserves the right to refuse any refunds or exchanges if all of the above conditions are not met. 
 All refunds will be issued to the original payment method.
 Discounts are only transferable when exchanging for the same item in different sizes or colors.
 Exchanges must be submitted accurately. Please ship the items specifically indicated with the return/exchange that’s been submitted.
 Questions about warranties?   See our  warranty policy  or  contact us .
 Exclusions 
 Final sale items are non-refundable but may be exchanged for a different size/color pending stock levels.
 All pro deals are final sale.
 How long does the return process take? 
 Most returns arrive back within five to seven business days of being shipped. We’ll do our best to process your return and issue a refund within three business days of reception. Refunds may take up to two weeks to be available in your account due to transit times and banking processes.
 International Returns 
 International returns are accepted as long as items meet the above criteria. Eligible items will be issued a refund to the original payment method or store credit; exchanges are not available due to customs regulations.
 Ridge will not credit shipping fees, customs and/or duties paid previously;  these must be pursued by customers within their own jurisdictions.
 International return shipping is NOT included with purchase.
