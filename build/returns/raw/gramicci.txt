 Refund policy
 Return and Exchange Policy 
 We want you to love your Gramicci product. This is why we offer a 30-day return policy for items purchased directly on the Gramicci.com website with options to return for an exchange, gift card, or refund to your original payment form. This policy covers unworn, unwashed items returned in original packaging, with tags attached, and free of damage and scents by the customer. 
 Please note that returns are not available for the following products: 
 Final Sale discounted items 
 Items purchased from somewhere other than Gramicci.com 
 Free gifts or promotional items with retail value 
 Returns received in damaged/worn condition 
 Returns received after 30 days of the original shipment date 
 Digital product such as gift cards 
 Start a Return 
 Return Shipping Label 
 Returns for   shoppers in the United States incur a $9 shipping label fee , which is deducted from your refund once your return has been processed. Exchanges for   shoppers in the United States are free of charge . Start a return or exchange at our   returns portal .   Shoppers who return from outside of the US may also use the   returns portal   to initiate a return and generate a return shipping label. However, please note that   international shoppers will be required to pay for shipping labels at their own expense . 
 Should you choose to return an item using your own method, we highly recommend including insurance and shipment tracking as we assume no responsibility for items that are lost or damaged in transit back to our receiving warehouse. 
 Refunds to Original Payment Method and Gift Cards 
 If you are requesting a refund, the average processing time is up to one week from the date the return arrives at our warehouse. We’ll be sure to send update emails to keep you informed on the status of your return including notification of your refund to the original payment method. 
 Exchanges 
 We kindly request that you drop off your return shipment as quickly as possible in order to ensure the item you selected for exchange does not go out of stock and to expedite shipment of your replacement Gramicci product. If you exchange for a replacement item of lower price, you will have an option to receive your partial refund via gift card or back to your original payment method. If you opt for an exchange for a higher priced product, you will be required to pay the difference at the time your exchange is processed for fulfillment (shortly after you drop off the returned item for shipment). Instructions for paying the difference will be emailed to the email address we have on file. 
 I did not buy my items directly from Gramicci.com 
 Our return policy covers only items purchased directly through Gramicci.com. Please return items purchased through third party retailers to the place of purchase. If you are unable to return the product to the original place of purchase, please call our corporate office at 1-800-814-5000 for assistance.

 Gift Card Terms and Conditions 
 Gift Cards are redeemable solely for online purchases on   Gramicci.com   and not on any of our other regional sites:   Gramicci.jp ,   Gramicci.co.uk ,   Gramicci.tw ,   m.Gramicci.kr , etc. 
 Gift Cards are   non-refundable . Lost or stolen Gift Cards will not be canceled and replaced without the required proof of purchase. Contact our customer service team for details. 
 Recipients of Gift Cards who shop online and ship products internationally, must shop on   Gramicci.com . We only ship to the locations specified in our   International Shipping Policy . 
 Gift Cards are   not   redeemable for cash. If the value of your Gift Card is greater than the amount of your total purchase, you will be able to use the remaining Gift Card balance for future purchases, subject to these terms and conditions. If the value of your Gift Card is less than the amount of your total   Gramicci.com   purchase, you must use a credit card in order to complete your purchase. 
 Purchase or use of any Gramicci Gift Card means you accept these terms and conditions. Any term of this Gift Card purchase agreement deemed unenforceable shall be severable and the remaining terms enforced. 
 Gift Cards are emailed individually to the specified email address at checkout within 24 hours. No special offers or discounts may be applied to the purchase of a Gift Card. Your Gift Card will never expire. 
 After your purchase, you will receive an order confirmation email and then a second email with your eGift card. We recommend placing your gift card email in a safe place and writing down your gift card number in case your email is lost. We can resend your gift card to the original email address if it is lost.
